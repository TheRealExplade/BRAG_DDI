# Dyclonine -- FDA label excerpts

_Source: openFDA (api.fda.gov), U.S. FDA structured product label._

Dosage And Administration: Directions for adults and children 2 years of age and older clean the affected area apply a small amount of this product on the area 1 to 3 times daily and let dry may be covered with a sterile bandage if bandaged, let dry first children under 2 years of age: do not use, consult a doctor
