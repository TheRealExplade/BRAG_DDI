# Capsaicin -- FDA label excerpts

_Source: openFDA (api.fda.gov), U.S. FDA structured product label._

Dosage And Administration: Directions Adults and children 18 years of age and older: Apply a thin film of cream to affected area and gently rub in until fully absorbed unless treating hands. Wash hands thoroughly with soap and water immediately after application for best results. Apply 3 to 4 times daily. Children under 18 years : Ask a doctor
