# Magnesium Salicylate -- FDA label excerpts

_Source: openFDA (api.fda.gov), U.S. FDA structured product label._

Dosage And Administration: DOSAGE & ADMINISTRATION Directions drink a full glass of water with each dose Adults and children: (12 years and over) Take 2 caplets every 6 hours, not to exceed 8 caplets in 24 hours. Children under 12 years: Do not use unless directed by a doctor
