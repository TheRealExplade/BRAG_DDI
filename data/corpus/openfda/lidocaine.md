# Lidocaine -- FDA label excerpts

_Source: openFDA (api.fda.gov), U.S. FDA structured product label._

Dosage And Administration: Adults: when practical, cleanse area with mild soap and warm water and rinse thoroughly. Gently dry by patting or blotting with toilet tissue or soft cloth before applying. Adults and children 12 years and older: apply externally to the affected area up to 4 times a day. Children under 12 years of age: consult a doctor.
